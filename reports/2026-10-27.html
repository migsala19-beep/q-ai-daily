🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：Agent 从"完成任务"走向"自我改进与持久学习"。arXiv 论文《Don't Forget What You Just Learned》提出自导向修复机制，让 Agent 不只纠正当前错误，还把失败转化为可复用经验；Phronix 进一步主张以"能力"而非"单次任务"为中心构建学习范式，减少对特定 harness 的依赖。二者共同指向：记忆、反思与技能沉淀将是下一代 Agent 的核心竞争力。

趋势二：AI 参与科研严谨性校验。《PaperFormalizer》用 Lean agentic pipeline 自动发现论文证明漏洞并补齐假设，把研究成果转成机器可验证版本。这意味着 AI 不止生成内容，还介入"正确性"环节，长期可能重塑数学与科研协作流程。
English
Trend 1 — Agents are moving from execution to self-improvement. The paper "Don't Forget What You Just Learned" introduces self-directed repair that converts mistakes into durable lessons, while Phronix argues for a capability-centric learning paradigm instead of task-specific harnesses. Together they signal that memory, reflection, and skill accumulation will define next-generation agents.

Trend 2 — AI is entering research verification. PaperFormalizer uses a Lean-based agentic pipeline to detect gaps in proofs, fill missing assumptions, and turn papers into machine-verified artifacts. Beyond content generation, AI is starting to own the "correctness" layer, potentially transforming math and scientific workflows.

English
1. Build an agent "lesson bank": vectorize past failures and retrieve them before similar tasks, inspired by self-directed repair.
2. Self-host internal AI tooling with Coolify: cut SaaS dependence and data egress risk while lowering long-term cost.
3. Maintain a versioned internal prompt library: adopt the prompts.chat community model for team-wide reuse.
4. Explore Lean/formal methods for critical checks: add machine-verifiable constraints to core algorithms and compliance docs.
5. Add tone/PCL screening to support and copy: detect condescending language early to protect brand trust.
