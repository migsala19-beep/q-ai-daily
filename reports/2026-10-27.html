🔬 深度研究报告

方向一：智能体的「持续学习」范式。今日两篇论文指向同一趋势——让Agent从被动执行转向主动进化。Phronix提出以能力为中心的学习范式：针对科学计算中的性能工程任务（代码插桩、分析、工作流移植），Agent不再依赖固定的外部harness，而是围绕自身能力缺口自主学习、补齐短板。另一篇"Don't Forget What You Just Learned"更进一步，提出自导向修复（self-directed repair）机制，将Agent犯的错误转化为持久经验教训，避免在同一处反复跌倒。这标志着Agent研究正从「Prompt工程」转向「学习机制设计」。

方向二：AI驱动的形式化验证进入科研流程。PaperFormalizer构建了基于Lean的智能体流水线，采用「作者在环」（author-in-the-loop）方式，帮助作者发现并补齐论文证明中的微小漏洞，最终产出经Lean机器验证的版本。即使是仔细校对的论文也可能存在假设缺失，形式化验证把严谨性检查从纯人工转向人机协同。此类工具若成熟，可能深刻改变论文评审与发表流程。
English
Trend 1: The "continual learning" paradigm for agents. Two of today's papers point to the same shift—from passive execution to active self-improvement. Phronix proposes a capability-centric learning paradigm: for scientific-computing performance engineering (instrumentation, analysis, workflow porting), agents no longer depend on a fixed external harness but learn autonomously around their own capability gaps. "Don't Forget What You Just Learned" goes further with self-directed repair, converting agent mistakes into lasting lessons so failures are not repeated. This signals a move from prompt engineering toward learning-mechanism design.

Trend 2: AI-driven formal verification enters the research workflow. PaperFormalizer builds a Lean-based agentic pipeline with an author-in-the-loop approach, helping authors find and close subtle gaps in proofs and ultimately produce Lean-verified versions of their papers. Even carefully proofread papers can hide missing assumptions; formalization shifts rigor checking from purely manual to human-machine collaboration. If mature, such tools could reshape how papers are reviewed and published.
