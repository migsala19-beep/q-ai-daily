🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：Agentic AI 从"堆算力"转向"可控验证与上下文工程"。今日多篇arXiv论文指向同一方向：《Verification and Self-Improvement in Agentic AI》指出单纯看性能分数无法区分智能体是靠"搜索更久、外部支持还是修改验证方式"取胜，提出用隐藏终端状态的边界验证来拆解改进机制；《Agent-Controlled Forgetting》则让模型自主决定将哪些历史工具调用结果替换为简短备注，实现可逆的上下文整理。这表明行业共识正在形成：上下文窗口是最稀缺的推理资源，"会忘"比"记得多"更重要。

趋势二：企业场景Agent训练遭遇数据瓶颈，仿真合成成为解法。《Synthesis Through Simulation》通过可扩展的Agent-系统交互生成连贯的企业表格数据，绕开商业与法律对真实数据库的限制；《Header-Centric STI》则从元数据层解决知识图谱入库前的质量评估问题。两者一训练一质检，共同指向"企业Agent落地基础设施"这一赛道。
English
Trend 1: Agentic AI is shifting from brute-force scaling to controllable verification and context engineering. "Verification and Self-Improvement in Agentic AI" argues that performance scores cannot distinguish whether gains come from longer search, external support, or modified verification—and proposes bounded verification with hidden terminal states to disentangle these mechanisms. "Agent-Controlled Forgetting" lets the model itself decide which tool-call results to replace with short notes, enabling reversible context curation. The emerging consensus: context is the scarcest reasoning resource, and forgetting well matters more than remembering everything.

Trend 2: Enterprise agent training faces data bottlenecks, and simulation-based synthesis is the workaround. "Synthesis Through Simulation" generates coherent enterprise data via scalable agent-system interactions, bypassing business/legal restrictions on real schemas, while the header-centric STI framework audits metadata quality before knowledge-graph ingestion—one for training, one for quality assurance, together pointing to the "enterprise agent infrastructure" track.

💡 实践启发

1. 给自己的Agent加"可逆遗忘"层——论文证明历史工具结果可压缩为短备注，直接省Token降成本。
2. 评估Agent别只看分数——引入隐藏状态的验证任务，区分搜索/支持/验证三种改进来源。
3. 缺企业数据时用仿真生成——法律拿不到的schema，可用Agent交互合成，自建训练集。
4. 表格数据入库前做header级语义审计——低成本拦截脏数据进入知识图谱。
5. 关注"Harness（运行壳）"设计——把合规与修改面集中在Harness层，模型本体保持稳定。
English
1. Add a reversible-forgetting layer to your agents—history tool results can be compressed into short notes, saving tokens and cost.
2. Don't judge agents by scores alone—use hidden-state verification tasks to separate search, support, and verification gains.
3. Use simulation when real enterprise data is legally unavailable—synthesize coherent schemas via agent interaction for self-built training sets.
4. Audit table headers semantically before KG ingestion—cheap metadata checks block dirty data early.
5. Watch the "harness" design space—concentrating compliance and mutability in the harness keeps the model core stable.
