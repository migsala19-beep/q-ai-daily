今日有效信息主要来自 arXiv。第一条线是稀疏 MoE 的工程化：LRMoE 提出需求侧负载再校准，针对专家路由不均导致的计算与通信瓶颈；相比继续增加专家副本或做静态放置，它更像在受限节点上重排“谁来算、何时算”，说明 MoE 竞争正从“堆容量”转向“服务成本与延迟可控”。第二条线是评估与决策可靠性：ThinkJev 关注封闭选项型决策，单次前向虽快，但对多步算术等任务概率常不可靠，需要按预算生成中间思考；OpenFrontiers 则把评估推向开放科研问题与“再发现”能力，警惕静态题库饱和和数据泄漏。共同启示：下一代模型能力不只看出分，还看校准、成本、可复现研究过程。
English
Today’s usable signal is mostly from arXiv. One trend is engineering sparse MoE under constraints: LRMoE reframes uneven expert routing as a demand-side load re-calibration problem, shifting MoE from raw capacity scaling toward controllable latency, communication, and memory cost on constrained nodes. A second trend is calibrated decision-making and harder evaluation: ThinkJev argues that typed decisions—closed-set choices whose probabilities downstream code consumes—cannot always be read from one forward pass, especially for multi-step arithmetic, and should spend inference budget deliberately. OpenFrontiers pushes benchmarking toward open research problems and scientific rediscovery, where models must reproduce discovery processes rather than recall answers. Together they suggest progress will be judged by calibration, efficiency, and auditable research behavior, not only benchmark scores.
