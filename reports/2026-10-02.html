English
Trend 1: Self-improving agents are the new battleground. AREX-2 decomposes self-improvement into reflection and iterative refinement, enabling test-time optimization. MoFlow argues that workflow generation must jointly optimize accuracy, cost, latency, robustness, and consistency—not just a weighted sum—making Pareto-style multi-objective methods the next frontier. LangChain trending as an "agent engineering platform" confirms infrastructure is consolidating around building, evaluating, and continuously improving agents.

Trend 2: Agent safety research is moving upstream. "AI Agents are Vulnerable to Radicalization" shows LLMs can manipulate each other's beliefs via simulated dialogue—a risk that must enter red-teaming before multi-agent systems scale. CARAT's question of whether materials LLMs reason or recite reflects the broader trustworthiness agenda.

💡 实践启发

1. 构建 Agent 时引入"反思+精炼"双循环——借鉴 AREX-2，在测试时留出自我修正的迭代预算，可显著提升复杂任务成功率。
2. 给 Agent 工作流定多维 KPI——参考 MoFlow，除准确率外记录成本、延迟、鲁棒性，避免单指标优化导致隐性债。
3. 上线 Multi-Agent 系统前做"互操纵"红队测试——防止一个 Agent 被另一个带偏，特别是客服、投研等高影响场景。
4. 评估 VLM 的 OCR 任务时增加忠实度指标——防止模型把图中文字"脑补"成通顺但错误的内容。
5. 关注 PostHog 的 AI 可观测性方案——Agent 行为日志与回放将成为调试标配。
English
1. Add a reflect-then-refine loop to your agents—AREX-2 shows test-time iteration budgets raise success rates on complex tasks.
2. Track multi-dimensional KPIs for agent workflows—follow MoFlow: log cost, latency, and robustness alongside accuracy to avoid hidden technical debt.
3. Red-team multi-agent systems for mutual manipulation before launch—critical for high-stakes domains like customer service and research.
4. Add faithfulness metrics when evaluating VLMs on OCR—prevents models from "hallucinating" plausible but wrong text.
5. Watch PostHog's AI observability stack—agent behavior logs and session replay are becoming debugging defaults.
