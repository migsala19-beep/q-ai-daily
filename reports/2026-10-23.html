🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：AI for Science 从"辅助工具"走向"自动化发现闭环"。Agent4Sci（arXiv:2610.28763）直接检验前沿编码智能体能否在真实 ML-for-Science 场景中自主爬坡、优化科学模型——这类任务需要超出常规 ML 工程的领域洞察，是 Agent 能力的试金石。SpecEvo（arXiv:2610.28706）则正面进攻成本瓶颈：不再对所有试验均匀施加昂贵推理，而是推测式地只进化高潜力分支，并从失败、冗余、饱和的试验中复用证据。两者共同说明：科研的"假设—试验—反思"闭环正被 LLM 接管，成本效率成为核心竞争点。

趋势二：Agent 基础设施开始自我进化。Agentic Harness Evolution 论文提出"用更多任务锻造更好执行框架"，让智能体框架随任务迭代自我改进；RAG 查询澄清工作（arXiv:2610.28686）表明"先问清楚再检索"能显著提升多轮对话下的检索质量，小模型即可胜任。配合虚拟世界中具身智能体的持续预训练（arXiv:2610.28698），"环境适应 + 自我改进"成为智能体新范式。
English
Trend 1 — AI for Science is shifting from assistance to autonomous discovery. Agent4Sci (arXiv:2610.28763) tests whether frontier coding agents can climb scientific hills in the wild, where improving a model demands domain insight beyond routine ML engineering. SpecEvo (arXiv:2610.28706) attacks the cost barrier: instead of applying expensive LLM reasoning uniformly, it speculatively evolves only high-potential branches while reusing evidence from failed, redundant, and saturated trials. Together they signal that the hypothesis–experiment–reflection loop of research is being delegated to LLM agents, with cost-efficiency as the binding constraint.

Trend 2 — Agent infrastructure is learning to improve itself. The Agentic Harness Evolution paper shows agentic frameworks can be forged by tasks into better harnesses, while the query-clarification study (arXiv:2610.28686) proves a small fine-tuned model disambiguates RAG queries better than brute-force retrieval. Combined with continued pre-training of embodied agents across diverse virtual worlds (arXiv:2610.28698), the pattern is clear: agents that adapt to environments and refine their own tooling define the new frontier.
