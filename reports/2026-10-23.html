🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：RAG 系统从"被动检索"走向"主动澄清"。论文《A Small Model Asks Better》提出用微调的小模型在检索前主动澄清歧义查询，显著优于直接用大模型硬答，启示是：在 RAG 流水线中插入一个轻量"澄清层"，成本可控且大幅提升多轮对话场景下的 grounding 质量。

趋势二：LLM 正在从"工具"进化为"科研协作者"。《SpecEvo》提出投机式进化搜索，只对高潜力试验调用昂贵推理，降低冗余成本；《Agent4Sci》则系统评估前沿 coding agent 能否在真实 ML4Science 任务中自主改进模型。两篇论文共同指向：agentic AI for science 从概念验证进入工程化阶段，成本与自治能力是下一个主战场。
English
Trend 1: RAG shifts from passive retrieval to proactive clarification. "A Small Model Asks Better" shows a fine-tuned small model can disambiguate queries before retrieval, outperforming brute-force large-model answers at lower cost. Practical takeaway: insert a lightweight "clarification layer" into RAG pipelines for multi-turn grounding quality.

Trend 2: LLMs evolve from tools into scientific collaborators. SpecEvo introduces speculative evolution, spending expensive reasoning only on promising trials, while Agent4Sci benchmarks whether frontier coding agents can autonomously improve scientific ML models. Together they mark the industrialization of agentic AI-for-science, where cost efficiency and autonomy are the next battlegrounds.
