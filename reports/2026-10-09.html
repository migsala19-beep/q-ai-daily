English
Two threads show LLM agents maturing from "works" to "trustworthy and evaluable." First, skill reuse is entering a sober phase: An Empirical Study of Agent Skills' Downstream Utility (arXiv:2610.08875) finds that packaging procedural guidance into skills does not reliably lift performance; utility depends on task, context, and invocation—echoing Adaptive Workflow Intelligence (2610.08793), which argues for context-driven cognitive architectures to keep enterprise automation robust under non-stationary policies and delayed feedback. Second, reliability is being procedure-conditioned: Route-Verify-Vote (2610.08814), evaluated on SCoRE 2026, upgrades self-consistency from plain majority voting to route–verify–vote conditioned on the procedure, enabling compositional generalization across unfamiliar mixed domains. Contextually, How Could AI Eliminate Humanity? catalogs civilizational failure modes, signaling that agent engineering must stay coupled to risk analysis. Business takeaway: tools and platforms that can prove when skills help—and how to invoke them—are the next high-value layer.
