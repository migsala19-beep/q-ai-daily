🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：AI 生产力的度量从“秒表”转向“账本”。Counting the Bandages 区分两种视角：秒表观只看时钟节省，账本观则把省下的时间放在“放行错误/返工”旁边对照核算——AI 越快，若缺陷漏出越多，净收益可能为负。这与 Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It 呼应：当 AI 在部分科学任务上强于人类，人类价值应转向提出问题、设计验证、承担责任，而非比拼产出速度。落地含义是：评估 Copilot/Agent 必须同时记录 cycle time 与 defect escape rate。

趋势二：Agent 工程化从“一次性生成”走向“可演进、可修正”。The Trinity 提出能力驱动的递归自我改进，但指出两大瓶颈：异构可验证环境构建昂贵、人工 Harness 迭代低效。CohortFlow 给出互补范式：用自然语言“转向”把修正表达为局部语义更新，避免重复处理未变需求，适合 eligibility criteria 频繁变化的临床队列。再看垂直场景，Olive Tree Diseases 用数据生成策略补齐数据不足与类别不平衡，说明小领域也能靠“合成数据+轻量模型”获得可用精度。综合看，护城河不在单次 prompt，而在评测环境、增量更新机制与领域数据管线。
English
Two trends stand out. First, how we measure AI productivity is shifting from a stopwatch to a ledger. Counting the Bandages argues that “time saved” should be read counterfactually next to the errors the work lets through—faster output can still be net-negative once defect rework is priced in. This pairs with Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It: when AI outperforms people on parts of science, human contribution moves toward problem selection, verification design, and accountability rather than raw speed. Practically, Copilot/Agent ROI should track cycle time alongside defect escape rate.

Second, agents are becoming evolvable and steerable rather than one-shot. The Trinity frames capability-driven recursive self-improvement, while naming two bottlenecks: costly construction of heterogeneous, verifiable environments and manual harness iteration. CohortFlow complements this by turning natural-language corrections into localized semantic updates so unchanged requirements are not reprocessed—ideal when eligibility criteria keep shifting. In vertical domains, the olive-tree-disease work shows synthetic data generation can offset scarce labels and class imbalance. The moat is shifting from prompts to evaluation environments, incremental-update mechanisms, and domain data pipelines.

💡 实践启发

- 给AI任务加“质量闸门”：理由——省时须扣除漏错返工，账本观更真实。
- 建立轻量评测集与回归Harness：理由——The Trinity表明可验证环境是自我改进瓶颈。
- 让业务Agent支持“只改差异”的局部更新：理由——CohortFlow思路可省重复计算与token。
- 垂直场景优先做数据合成与重采样：理由——小样本/失衡下，数据策略常比换模型更有效。
- 把人类岗位说明书改成“选题+验证+担责”：理由——AI更强时，人类优势在判断与责任。
English
- Add a quality gate to AI tasks: saved time must be net of escaped defects and rework.
- Build a lightweight eval set plus regression harness: verifiable environments are the self-improvement bottleneck.
- Let business agents apply localized, diff-only updates: CohortFlow-style changes save recompute and tokens.
- In niche domains, prioritize data generation and reweighting: it often beats swapping models under scarcity and imbalance.
- Rewrite human roles toward problem choice, verification, and accountability: that is where people stay decisive as AI improves.
