🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：AI 效能评估进入"账本视角"时代。《Counting the Bandages》一文指出，AI 节省的时间不能只看计时器（stopwatch），而要用"账本视角"（ledger）——将节省时间与被放行的工作错误对照衡量。这揭示了企业落地 AI 的一个核心痛点：局部提速可能带来全局返工成本。对管理者而言，评估 AI 工具 ROI 需要引入质量审计指标。

趋势二：智能体自我进化与垂直领域落地并进。《The Trinity》探索多模态 Agent 的能力驱动递归自我改进，试图突破"环境构建昂贵、人工迭代瓶颈"两大障碍；与此同时 CohortFlow 将这一范式落地医疗场景，通过局部化语义修正实现"增量式"队列筛选，避免重复处理未变更的需求。两者共同指向：Agent 设计的下一战场是"可持续迭代"而非"一次性性能"。
English
Trend 1: AI efficiency evaluation enters the "ledger view" era. The paper "Counting the Bandages" argues that time saved by AI must be measured counterfactually, alongside the errors such work lets through — not merely by the stopwatch. This reveals a core pain point in enterprise AI adoption: local speedups may cause global rework costs. Managers should incorporate quality-audit metrics when evaluating AI tool ROI.

Trend 2: Agent self-evolution and vertical-domain deployment advance together. "The Trinity" explores capability-driven recursive self-improvement for multimodal agents, targeting two bottlenecks — costly environment construction and manual iteration. Meanwhile, CohortFlow lands this paradigm in healthcare, using localized semantic correction for incremental cohort curation. Both point to the same conclusion: the next battlefield in agent design is sustainable iteration, not one-shot performance.

- 小数据领域可借鉴数据生成策略——垂直行业（农业/医疗）冷启动成本低
- 医疗/科研团队关注语义修正型 Agent——减少重复处理未变更需求
English
- Add a "rework rate" metric when evaluating AI tools — speed without quality is a false economy
- Adopt CohortFlow-style localized correction design — reusable in scenarios with frequently changing business requirements
- Leverage data generation strategies in low-data domains — lowers cold-start cost for vertical industries like agriculture and healthcare
- Medical/research teams should watch semantic-correction agents — they avoid reprocessing unchanged requirements
