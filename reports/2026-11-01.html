趋势一：MoE 的效率工程化。LRMoE 针对稀疏混合专家模型的负载不均问题，提出需求侧负载重校准方案，在不增加专家副本的前提下缓解计算与通信瓶颈。这与近期业界（如 DeepSeek、Mixtral 系列）对 MoE 落地成本的持续压榨一脉相承——MoE 的竞争已从"能不能做"转向"能不能便宜地做"。

趋势二：决策校准与评估基准。ThinkJev 关注一个被忽视的场景：LLM 输出的概率若直接驱动代码决策（如算术、多步推理），单次前向传播的概率往往失真，论文探索了"一次思考、任意预算"的校准方法。OpenFrontiers 则构建了开放科研问题集，用"科学再发现"来评估 LLM 的真实研究能力，弥补了现有基准多停留在解题层面的空白。

两者共同说明：社区正从"模型更大"转向"输出更可信、评估更真实"。
English
Two trends emerge from today's sparse feed, both centered on reliability under constraints.

First, efficiency engineering for MoE. LRMoE addresses uneven expert routing in sparse Mixture-of-Experts models by recalibrating demand-side load, easing compute and communication bottlenecks without extra expert replicas. This reflects the broader industry shift — seen in DeepSeek and Mixtral lineages — from "can MoE work" to "can MoE be run cheaply on memory-constrained hardware."

Second, decision calibration and honest evaluation. ThinkJev targets a neglected scenario: when LLM probabilities directly drive code-level decisions, single-pass outputs are often miscalibrated on multi-step tasks, and the paper proposes amortized inference that adapts to any reasoning budget. OpenFrontiers complements this with a benchmark of open research problems, measuring "scientific rediscovery" to probe whether LLMs can do genuine research rather than pattern-matched problem solving.

Together, they signal a maturing field: less focus on raw scale, more on trustworthiness and truthful evaluation.
