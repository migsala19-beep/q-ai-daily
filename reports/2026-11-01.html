Two threads stand out today despite thin feeds.

Meanwhile, Amortized Localized Posterior Inference tackles scientific model calibration for ocean turbulence closures, jointly handling observation noise and context uncertainty — a notable advance in physics-informed ML.

Second, the push toward research-grade AI and scalable agents continues. OpenFrontiers curates open research problems across disciplines to benchmark whether LLMs can rediscover known scientific results, filling a gap left by math/coding benchmarks. WebAnything converts the wild web into a scalable agent environment, echoing industry bets on web-operating agents. Finally, LRMoE attacks MoE load imbalance from the demand side rather than expert replication, a design choice that could reshape distributed MoE training on memory-constrained hardware.
