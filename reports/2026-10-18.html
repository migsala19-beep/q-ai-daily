🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：智能体（Agent）评测正从"静态榜单"转向"动态、可验证、带时钟"的真实环境度量。RealtimeBench 提出 180 个任务、横跨六类游戏场景，核心洞察是：大模型智能体的推理与工具调用耗时不可忽视，在持续变化的环境中，"正确但迟到"的动作等同于失败。AlphaDiana 则构建了面向可验证推理任务的智能体测量系统，把执行条件、行为轨迹与资源消耗关联起来。配合《Evaluating Model Capabilities After Benchmark Saturation》一文指出的"基准饱和使榜单失去区分度"问题，可以看出社区正在重建评估基础设施——这对 AI 安全论证与前沿政策制定至关重要。

趋势二：测试时对齐（test-time alignment）的计算效率成为优化焦点。Value-Informed Schedule Trimming 论文针对离散扩散的 SMC 引导，只在高价值去噪步施加奖励引导，避免每步都调用奖励模型，大幅降低计算开销。这反映了一个共识：测试时计算是稀缺资源，值得"精打细算"地调度。
English
Trend 1: Agent evaluation is shifting from static leaderboards to dynamic, verifiable, clock-aware measurement. RealtimeBench (180 tasks, six game types) shows that in continuously changing environments, a correct action that arrives too late is worthless—agent latency must be a first-class metric. AlphaDiana builds a measurement system linking execution conditions, behavior, and resource use for verifiable reasoning tasks. Together with the benchmark-saturation paper, this signals a rebuild of evaluation infrastructure for AI safety cases and frontier policy.

Trend 2: Efficient test-time alignment is a key optimization frontier. The SMC schedule-trimming paper applies reward guidance only at high-value denoising steps in discrete diffusion, cutting reward-model calls dramatically. The broader signal: test-time compute is scarce and must be scheduled deliberately.
