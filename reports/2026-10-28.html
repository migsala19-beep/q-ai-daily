Trend 1: The self-hosting infrastructure movement is gaining real momentum. Coolify (open-source, self-hostable alternative to Vercel/Heroku/Netlify) topped GitHub Trending, alongside RustDesk (self-hosted TeamViewer alternative) and VictoriaMetrics (self-hosted monitoring). Trend 2: AI agents are evolving from "completing tasks" to "continuous learning." Two arXiv papers today point the same direction: Phronix proposes a capability-centric learning paradigm, arguing agent effectiveness depends on accumulated capabilities rather than one-shot prompt engineering; "Don't Forget What You Just Learned" introduces self-directed repair that turns agent mistakes into lasting lessons. Together with PaperFormalizer (author-in-the-loop autoformalization), this marks a shift from harness engineering toward long-term memory, self-improvement, and verifiability as the next agent core competencies.
