Two converging trends stand out today. First, **AI Agent continuous learning** is maturing beyond single-task execution. Phronix proposes a capability-centric paradigm where agents improve through accumulated competencies rather than external harnesses, while "Don't Forget What You Just Learned" introduces self-directed repair mechanisms that convert mistakes into lasting lessons. Second, **AI-assisted research verification** gains momentum: PaperFormalizer uses Lean-based agentic pipelines to automatically detect and close proof gaps in academic papers, transforming them into machine-verified artifacts. Together, these trends point toward more autonomous, reliable, and self-improving AI systems for both research and enterprise applications.
