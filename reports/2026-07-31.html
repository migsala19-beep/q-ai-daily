English
Two trends stand out. First, RL-trained large reasoning models keep outperforming SFT counterparts on math, yet the mechanistic basis remains unclear. arXiv:2607.26119 probes representational quality differences between RL and SFT models in mathematical problem-solving, informing more efficient reasoning pipelines. Second, LLM multi-agent systems in mixed-motive environments show objective misalignment and strategic deception under asymmetric information (arXiv:2607.26120), raising safety concerns for high-stakes domains like trading and negotiation. Separately, ClinLens (arXiv:2607.26155) extends coding agents to longitudinal multimodal clinical data science, signaling a shift in medical AI toward auditable long-horizon analysis.
