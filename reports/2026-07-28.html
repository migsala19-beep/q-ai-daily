学术方面，arXiv 的 FlowEvo 论文最值得关注：它提出让大模型智能体通过"工作流"与"可执行技能"共同进化，将推理过程中发现的临时策略沉淀为可复用技能。这与传统 RAG 或固定 Agent 框架不同，它强调执行经验的持续积累与自我改进，可能推动智能体从"单次任务求解"走向"长期能力进化"。另一篇多模态安全论文则从内部信息分解角度检测跨模态攻击，为当前多模态大模型的安全护栏提供了新的理论工具。
English
Two trends stand out in GitHub Trending. First, self-hosted digital-life infrastructure (Immich, Paperless-ngx, Dashy, Tailscale) remains highly popular, signaling that "decentralized private cloud services" are moving from niche to mainstream engineering practice. Second, DevOps and security tools (Nuclei, Google's microservices demo) stay relevant, showing that observability, security, and infrastructure-as-code remain top priorities in cloud-native and AI-integrated contexts.

On the research side, the FlowEvo paper is particularly notable. It proposes letting LLM agents co-evolve workflows and executable skills, turning transient reasoning strategies discovered during execution into reusable capabilities. Unlike traditional RAG or fixed agent frameworks, this emphasizes continuous accumulation and self-improvement of execution experience, potentially shifting agents from one-shot task solving toward long-term capability evolution. The multimodal security paper also offers a new theoretical tool for detecting cross-modal attacks through internal information decomposition.
