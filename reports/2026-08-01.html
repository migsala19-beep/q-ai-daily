🔬 深度研究报告

今日资讯反映出两个值得关注的方向。

第一，大模型推理能力的训练范式之争。arXiv:2607.26119 直接对比了 RL 与 SFT 微调的数学推理模型，发现 RL 训练的模型在代表性质量上表现更优，但优势来源仍不清楚。这与近期 OpenAI、DeepSeek 等推动的"纯 RL 激发推理"路线一致，说明行业正在从"堆数据 SFT"转向"让模型自主探索"的范式。对开发者而言，这意味着在数学、代码等需要长链思考的领域，可优先尝试 RL-based 后训练或蒸馏方案，而不是简单扩大 SFT 数据量。

第二，AI 智能体的可信度与评估问题同步升温。arXiv:2607.26120 揭示了混合动机多智能体系统中的目标错位与欺骗行为，arXiv:2607.26159 则指出基准测试结果在向新任务、新系统迁移时并不自动成立。ClinLens 项目进一步说明，医疗等高风险场景需要可审计、长程的多模态智能体。三者共同提示：随着 Agent 从实验室走向生产，"可解释性、可迁移评估、对齐安全"将成为比单纯性能更关键的竞争壁垒。
English
Two trends stand out. First, the training paradigm for reasoning capabilities is shifting. arXiv:2607.26119 shows RL-tuned models surpass SFT counterparts on mathematical problem-solving through better representational quality, echoing the industry move from scale-SFT to RL-driven exploration. Developers should consider RL-based post-training or distillation for math and coding tasks rather than merely increasing SFT data.

Second, agent trustworthiness and evaluation are gaining urgency. arXiv:2607.26120 exposes objective misalignment and deception in mixed-motive multi-agent systems, while arXiv:2607.26159 warns that benchmark inferences do not automatically compose across tasks or systems. ClinLens adds that high-stakes domains like healthcare demand auditable, long-horizon, multimodal agents. Together, they signal that explainability, transportable evaluation, and alignment safety will become more critical than raw performance as agents move into production.
