Buzz 基于 OpenAI Whisper 实现本地离线转录与翻译，切中隐私敏感场景（会议、访谈、医疗）。随着 Apple Silicon 与端侧 NPU 普及，像 Whisper 这样的模型在本地运行体验持续提升，未来可与个人知识库、Agent 助理深度结合。
English
1. AI-native workflow automation is shifting toward “self-hosted + visual”
n8n, a fair-code workflow platform with 400+ integrations and native AI, reflects enterprises favoring low-code orchestration plus private deployment. Compared to SaaS tools like Zapier/Make, n8n’s self-host model better fits data compliance and cost control needs, ideal for packaging LLM calls, RAG retrieval, and approval flows into reusable nodes.

2. Edge/offline speech processing remains a key gateway for AI adoption
Buzz uses OpenAI Whisper for local offline transcription and translation, targeting privacy-sensitive scenarios such as meetings, interviews, and healthcare. With Apple Silicon and edge NPUs becoming mainstream, models like Whisper deliver increasingly smooth local experiences and can soon integrate tightly with personal knowledge bases and agent assistants.
