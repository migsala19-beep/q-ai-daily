English
The clearest signal today is the agent-native evolution of databases. Apache Doris now brands itself as a "real-time analytics and hybrid search database for AI agents," while ClickHouse also trends, showing that vector search and scalar analytics are converging into one engine to serve agent memory, RAG retrieval, and tool-call logging. RAG architectures may shift from bolt-on vector stores back to unified analytics engines, cutting consistency and ops overhead.

A second thread is the "invisible infrastructure" of AI engineering. Transformers trending again confirms the model-definition layer is standardized; meanwhile bun, UniGetUI, sniffnet, and Ghidra signal that real AI deployment is pulling in adjacent toolchains — faster runtimes, package management, traffic auditing, and reverse engineering. Ghidra's appearance is notable: AI-assisted reverse engineering and security analysis may be the next capability wave.
