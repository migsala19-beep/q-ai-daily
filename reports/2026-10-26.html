🔬 深度研究报告

今日信号虽少，但指向两条清晰主线。第一，AI应用正从“能跑”转向“可诊断、可运营”。PostHog把分析、会话回放、实验、错误追踪、日志与MCP入口放在同一产品叙事里，本质上是在回答：当产品越来越多由Agent参与生成、修复和决策时，团队如何持续理解线上行为、归因问题并闭环迭代。这对AI日报、客服、编程助手、数据分析Agent都有启发：上线不是终点，事件流、反馈样本、失败案例和工具调用轨迹才是下一轮模型与提示词优化的原料。第二，垂直领域研发自动化在增强。microsoft/qlib长期服务AI量化，覆盖监督学习、市场动态建模与强化学习；其描述中明确提到与RD-Agent结合，自动化R&D流程。这代表一种范式：不只预测一个数值，而是把假设生成、因子挖掘、回测、代码实验和结果归档连成流水线。相比通用AutoML，金融/量化场景因数据边界清晰、评价指标可回测，更适合先做“研发自动化样板”。
English
Two trends stand out. First, AI products are moving from demo quality to operability. PostHog frames analytics, session replay, experiments, error tracking, logs, and MCP access as one “self-driving product” stack. The implication is that once agents help generate, debug, and ship product changes, teams need continuous telemetry: event streams, failure traces, tool-call records, and user feedback become training and evaluation assets. This applies beyond product analytics to coding assistants, support agents, and report generators. Second, R&D automation is getting more vertical. microsoft/qlib is an AI-oriented quant platform spanning supervised learning, market dynamics modeling, and RL; its note about RD-Agent points to automating the research loop itself—hypothesis generation, feature/factor discovery, backtesting, experimentation, and result archival. Quant is an early proving ground because objectives are measurable and backtests are cheap. Expect similar loops in materials, bio, ads, and operations research.

English
- Add observability to AI features: without replay and failure traces, tuning becomes guesswork.
- Version-control prompts as assets: community prompt registries are becoming operational infrastructure.
- Build experiment pipelines in the Qlib/RD-Agent style: standardize backtests and archives before automating research.
- Evaluate self-hosted data and scheduling stacks: NocoDB, Cal.com, and Coolify reduce SaaS lock-in and privacy risk.
- Filter non-AI noise from daily briefings: game mod spikes should not dilute the core signal.
