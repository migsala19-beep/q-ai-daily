🔬 深度研究报告

今日值得关注的第一个方向是「生成模型的约束控制」。MintFlow 论文针对 flow matching 模型的约束采样问题提出最小轨迹干预方法，突破了现有方法在观测约束与物理定律之间"顾此失彼"的权衡。这对工业仿真、分子生成等需要"生成结果必须满足硬约束"的场景意义重大，意味着生成模型可以更可靠地嵌入工程 pipeline，而不必事后筛选。

第二个方向是「Agent 基础设施的快速决策层」。论文《Fast Models, Slow Evidence》对 System-1 决策模型（单次前向传播即可判断"该调哪个模型、该用哪个工具、检索文本是否相关、输入是否含注入"）做了配对自审计评估。随着 Agent 每秒要做数十个小型决策，在 harness 层引入轻量决策模型来降低成本和延迟，是一个被低估但即将爆发的工程方向。值得注意的是，今日 arXiv 还有一篇用 LLM 批量分析上市公司年报中的 AI 风险披露，说明"用 LLM 做大规模文本社会学研究"的方法论正在成熟。
English
Two trends stand out today. First, constrained control for generative models: MintFlow introduces minimal trajectory intervention for constrained flow matching, breaking the trade-off existing constrained samplers face between observed measurements and physical laws. This matters for industrial simulation and molecular generation, where samples must satisfy hard constraints natively rather than being filtered post-hoc. Second, a fast decision layer for agent infrastructure: "Fast Models, Slow Evidence" provides a paired, self-audited evaluation of System-1 decision models that answer agent-harness micro-decisions (model selection, tool choice, retrieval relevance, injection detection) in a single forward pass. As agents make dozens of small typed decisions per task, lightweight decision models at the harness layer are an underrated but emerging engineering direction. Meanwhile, the AI Risk Observatory paper shows LLM-powered large-scale analysis of corporate annual reports is maturing as a methodology.
