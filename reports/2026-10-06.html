🔬 深度研究报告

今日最值得关注的方向是“AI安全从能力演示转向可审计治理”。CALM 对 text-to-image 训练免费防护提出几何化质疑：若只学习一个全局“不安全方向”或 toxic subspace，再跨 prompt 复用，本质上假设不安全 token 在所有语境下共享稳定方向；这在组合 prompt、隐式概念和风格迁移中很可能失效，说明安全机制需要从“全局方向”升级为“条件依赖、按上下文重估”的局部信号。另一条线是社会韧性测量：AI Risk Observatory 用 LLM 规模化处理公司年报，分析企业如何披露 AI 风险与应对，试图把分散文本转成可比较信号。这对政策、投资尽调和供应链风险管理都有价值，但也提示风险：LLM 摘要可能放大数据漂移、模板化披露和“讲得多做得少”的偏差。工程层面，System-1 decision models 论文则提醒：agent harness 里大量小决策，如选模型、选工具、判相关性、识别注入，不应只看单次准确率，而要配对样本、自我审计、慢证据复核。综合来看，AI 正在从“能生成”进入“可约束、可解释、可披露”的阶段；真正壁垒不只是模型，而是评估协议、审计日志和风险口径。
English
The clearest thread today is the shift from AI capability demos toward auditable safety and governance. CALM challenges training-free safeguards for text-to-image models that rely on a single global unsafe direction or toxic subspace reused across prompts. Its geometric analysis implies that unsafety is likely conditional rather than globally stable, especially under compositional prompts, implicit concepts, and style transfer. The practical implication is that safety signals must be recomputed per context instead of applied as one reusable vector. A second signal comes from the AI Risk Observatory, which uses LLMs to process annual reports at scale and extract comparable disclosure patterns about corporate AI risk and resilience. This can support policy analysis, investment due diligence, and supply-chain risk monitoring, but it also carries bias risks: boilerplate disclosure, selective reporting, and LLM summarization drift may be mistaken for real resilience. Finally, the System-1 decision model paper argues that agent harnesses make many small but consequential decisions—model choice, tool choice, relevance, injection detection—and should be evaluated with paired samples and self-audited against slower evidence. Together, these papers point to a maturing AI stack where constraints, auditability, and disclosure quality matter as much as raw generation quality.

💡 实践启发

- 建一份“AI披露年报”监测表：跟踪头部客户/供应商如何描述AI风险，比新闻稿更接近真实落地。理由：披露文本是可规模化采集的一手信号。
- 不要把图像安全只压在全局 unsafe direction：上线前用组合prompt、反事实prompt做回归集。理由：CALM提示全局安全假设在复杂语境下易失效。
- Agent小决策加“慢证据”复核：高风险动作要求检索证据/二次模型确认。理由：单次前向分类快，但易被注入和上下文欺骗。
- 对约束生成做业务试点：如检测报告补全、传感器数据增强，用MintFlow思路满足物理/观测约束。理由：生成样本必须可控才有产业价值。
- Trending项目当工程基建看：Caddy、yt-dlp、Zettlr可用于采集、分发和写作流水线，但别误读为AI热度。理由：今日GitHub热点主要是工具链而非模型创新。
English
- Track an “AI disclosure in annual reports” sheet for key customers and vendors; it is closer to real adoption than press releases.
- Do not rely only on a global unsafe direction for image safety; test compositional and counterfactual prompts before release.
- Add slow-evidence checks to agent micro-decisions, especially tool calls, retrieval relevance, and injection detection.
- Pilot constrained generation for inspection reports or sensor-data augmentation where physical or observed constraints must hold.
- Treat Caddy, yt-dlp, and Zettlr as pipeline infrastructure, not as evidence of AI-native momentum today.
