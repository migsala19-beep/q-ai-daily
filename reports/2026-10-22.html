🔬 深度研究报告

今日 arXiv 密集出现"可验证任务执行 + 长程后训练"的 Agent 研究，标志 Agentic AI 从通用对话助手转向专业岗位替代。Navalia（arXiv:2610.26887）提出 Cowork Agents 概念，通过可验证任务合成与长程后训练，让 LLM Agent 自主完成需跨文档证据整合的金融分析；MedPAO-Assist（arXiv:2610.26913）进一步把 Agentic AI 压缩到边缘设备，实现放射报告结构化，摆脱对云端大模型与高端 GPU 的依赖，直击临床落地瓶颈。

另一主线是 RLVR（可验证奖励强化学习）向硬科技领域渗透。Pareto-Optimal RTL（arXiv:2610.26904）用多目标 RL 让 LLM 生成电路代码时同时优化功耗、性能、面积（PPA），替代人工权衡；LLM+RL 建筑施工论文（arXiv:2610.26899）则将同一范式用于工程进度自主更新。GitHub 侧 NVIDIA/Megatron-LM 持续大热，为这类大规模 RL 后训练提供底座；Apache Doris 明确瞄准"AI agents 的实时分析与混合搜索"，Supabase、Airflow 同列 Trending，印证数据基建仍是 AI 爆发的隐形赢家。
English
Today's arXiv drop signals a clear shift: Agentic AI is moving from chat assistants to verifiable, long-horizon task execution. Navalia (arXiv:2610.26887) introduces Cowork Agents trained via verifiable task synthesis for financial analysis requiring cross-document reasoning. MedPAO-Assist (arXiv:2610.26913) pushes agentic AI to edge devices for radiology report structuring, removing the cloud/GPU dependency that has blocked clinical adoption.

A second thread is RLVR (RL with Verifiable Rewards) expanding into hardware. Pareto-Optimal RTL (arXiv:2610.26904) applies multi-objective RL so LLMs generate circuits optimizing Power, Performance and Area simultaneously. On GitHub, NVIDIA/Megatron-LM stays hot as the training backbone, while Apache Doris positions itself as the real-time hybrid-search database "for AI agents" — alongside Supabase and Airflow, confirming data infrastructure as the quiet winner of the AI boom.
