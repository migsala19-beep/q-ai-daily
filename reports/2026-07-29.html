🔬 深度研究报告

今日两个核心方向值得关注。一是“智能体自进化”。arXiv 论文 FlowEvo 提出让大模型智能体在执行复杂任务时，同步演化推理工作流与可执行技能，使“推理—工具调用—代码执行”不再是静态模板，而是能在运行中自我改进的活系统。这标志着 Agent 开发正从“人工编排流程”走向“自动沉淀并复用程序性知识”。二是“多模态 AI 安全”。论文《Securing Multimodal AI through Internal Information Decomposition》指出，攻击者可将恶意意图拆分到文本、图像等不同模态，绕过单一模态护栏；作者建议对模型内部信息进行分解，以跨模态一致性作为异常检测信号。结合 GitHub 上 OpenAI Whisper 的持续热度，语音、文本、视觉的融合正在同步放大机会与攻击面，未来的多模态系统必须在能力扩展的同时配套动态安全机制。
English
Two key trends stand out: self-evolving agents and multimodal AI safety. The arXiv paper FlowEvo proposes co-evolving inference-time workflows and executable skills, turning agents from manually orchestrated pipelines into systems that discover and reuse procedural knowledge during execution. This could reduce hand-crafted prompt engineering and improve task generalization. On the safety side, "Securing Multimodal AI through Internal Information Decomposition" argues that multimodal threats can distribute adversarial intent across text, image, and other modalities, evading unimodal safeguards. The authors recommend decomposing internal model information and using cross-modal consistency as a detection signal. Together with Whisper's continued popularity in open-source ASR, the message is clear: as voice, text, and vision converge, agent capabilities will grow alongside attack surfaces, making runtime workflow evolution and cross-modal safety monitoring essential design priorities.

2. 为 multimodal 产品加入跨模态一致性校验：将文本、图像、语音的内部表征对齐分数作为风控输入，防御分布式对抗提示。
