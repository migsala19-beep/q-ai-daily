English
The dominant trend is self-evolving agent architectures. FlowEvo introduces co-evolution of workflows and executable skills, enabling LLM agents to discover reusable procedures during execution rather than relying on fixed prompts. This shifts focus from static engineering to dynamic, self-improving systems. At the same time, multimodal AI security research warns that attacks can distribute malicious intent across text, image, and audio, requiring cross-modal consistency checks rather than isolated per-modality filters. Complementing these advances, Whisper and GoogleCloudPlatform/microservices-demo indicate that reliable speech infrastructure and cloud-native patterns remain critical enablers for production AI systems.
