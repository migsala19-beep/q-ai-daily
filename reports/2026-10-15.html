🔬 深度研究报告

**趋势一：Agent 评估体系正在重构。** 传统基准面临"循环校准"困境——用人类或当前AI能力标定难度，很快就会饱和。《Artificiality's First Exam》提出 LLM 引用的 C-test，试图为"超越当前机器的智能"设计不依赖人类标准的考题；Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench 2.0 则自动化构建多样化长程任务，衡量 Agent 的持续进步而非单点表现。这说明行业正从"模仿人类能力"转向"测量自主进化潜力"。

**趋势二：Agent 自我改进与可靠性焦虑并存。** ClawLoop 展示了在沙箱工作区内通过文件系统、Shell 交互实现"原生训练闭环"的自我改进 Agent；但《The Silent Failures in Agentic Data Science》同时警示：当前数据科学 Agent 在任务形式化到模型开发全流程中存在大量未被检测到的隐性失败。技术前沿在狂奔，可靠性工程必须同步跟进，否则自动化程度越高，累积错误越难察觉。扩散模型的 Correction Training 同样指向这一问题——推理时的误差积累需要显式校正机制。
English
**Trend 1: The agent evaluation framework is being rebuilt.** Traditional benchmarks face circular calibration—difficulty anchored to humans or current AI saturates quickly. *Artificiality's First Exam* proposes an LLM-referenced C-test to evaluate intelligence beyond present machines without human standards, while *Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench 2.0* automates diverse long-horizon task construction to measure sustained progress. The field is shifting from "mimicking human ability" to "measuring autonomous evolution potential."

**Trend 2: Agent self-improvement runs parallel to reliability anxiety.** ClawLoop demonstrates harness-native training loops where agents self-improve through file system and shell interactions in sandbox workspaces. However, *The Silent Failures in Agentic Data Science* warns that current agents exhibit undetected failures across the entire pipeline—from task formulation to model development. Correction Training for diffusion models addresses analogous exposure bias. The frontier is accelerating, but reliability engineering must keep pace: higher automation amplifies invisible error accumulation.

💡 实践启发

1. **建立"静默失败"检查清单**——数据科学Agent论文提示我们，自动化流程越顺畅，越要强制设置人工审计节点与异常指标看板。
2. **评估Agent时改用长程任务基准**——短任务跑分已无法反映真实能力，参考 Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench 2.0 设计持续数小时/数天的复合任务测试内部Agent。
3. **扩散模型项目引入校正机制**——若你在做图像/视频生成，考虑在推理链中加入 Correction Training 思路，减少多步采样漂移。
4. **跟踪自我改进Agent技术栈**——ClawLoop 类原生训练闭环可能重塑后训练流程，建议提前在沙箱环境复现，评估对现有工作流的替代性。
English
1. **Build a silent-failure checklist**—The data science agent paper shows that smoother automation demands mandatory human audit nodes and anomaly dashboards.
2. **Adopt long-horizon benchmarks for internal agents**—Short-task scores no longer reflect real capability; design multi-hour/day composite tasks referencing Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench 2.0.
3. **Integrate correction mechanisms in diffusion projects**—If you work on image/video generation, apply Correction Training ideas to reduce multi-step sampling drift.
4. **Track self-improving agent stacks**—Harness-native training loops like ClawLoop may reshape post-training pipelines; reproduce early in sandbox environments to assess workflow disruption.
