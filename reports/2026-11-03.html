🔬 深度研究报告

今日最值得注意的不是单点突破，而是“Agent 评估与安全”正在从能力评测转向风险边界。论文《More Rules, Longer Lists?》指出，自进化 Agent 可能靠记录例外通过隐藏规则课程，列表变长并不等于真正学会规则；这提示评价自进化系统不能只看成功率，还要看状态是否膨胀、规则是否可泛化。另一个方向是 Web World Models 的安全充分性：模型能预测 plausible future，不代表能支撑正确安全决策。这对浏览器 Agent、自动下单、企业流程自动化尤其关键。工程侧，ONNX Runtime、build-your-own-x、Data-Science-For-Beginners 持续上榜，说明AI落地仍依赖推理加速、基础能力重建和数据科学普惠。
English
The main signal is a shift from capability benchmarking to safety and evaluation boundaries for agents. “More Rules, Longer Lists?” argues that self-evolving agents may pass hidden-rule curricula by accumulating exceptions rather than learning general rules, so persistent-state growth matters as much as success rate. Separately, “Beyond Plausible Prediction” shows that web world models can generate plausible futures while still failing safety-sufficient decisions, which is critical for browser agents and enterprise automation. On the engineering side, ONNX Runtime, build-your-own-x, and Microsoft’s data-science curriculum remain prominent, indicating that practical AI progress still depends on inference efficiency, foundational rebuilding, and broad data-science literacy.
