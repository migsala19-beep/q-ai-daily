Trend 1: Research on AI Agent safety and evaluation is surging. Three arXiv papers today send a clear signal. A third paper explores ChatGPT's risk as a surveillance assistant, showing that stronger capabilities widen misuse surfaces. Together: as agent capabilities leap, safety evaluation must evolve in lockstep.

Trend 2: AI dev infrastructure keeps democratizing. ONNX Runtime remains on GitHub Trending as a cross-platform inference accelerator, alongside Microsoft's Data-Science-For-Beginners and build-your-own-x, reflecting an unprecedented lowering of barriers for everyday developers. This infrastructure wave is the foundation for the agent application explosion above it.
