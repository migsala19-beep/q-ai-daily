English
All four inputs are empty today. Separate two causes before acting. “Genuinely empty” is plausible: arXiv cs.AI/cs.CL rarely posts on Sundays, HuggingFace Papers mirrors arXiv, GitHub Trending slows on weekends, and vendor blogs are not daily. “Silent fetch failure” is the risk: anti-bot blocks, timeouts, or rate limits can yield empty lists without errors. Discriminate by logging HTTP status, response size, and timestamps per request. Consistent 200s with tiny bodies suggest a true lull; 403/429/5xx or connect timeouts indicate faults and should trigger retries and alerts. Treat “empty” as a first-class state: surface a data-status badge in the report header and alert only after N consecutive empties from the same source. This beats reflexively adding more feeds and makes each source’s cadence observable and auditable.
