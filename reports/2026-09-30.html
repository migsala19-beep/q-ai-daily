🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：智能体向安全攸关领域渗透，"护栏内置"成为标配。今日 arXiv 多篇论文指向同一方向：SMARtCARE 提出有界自主（bounded-autonomy）的临床决策智能体，通过隐私保护架构让 ICU 监测 AI 利用完整病史，避免长上下文窗口外的既往恶化模式被误判为"非特异性"体征；CP-Agent 则将智能体工程化方法引入晶体塑性仿真，自动完成异构工具配置、多步数据管道编排与参数标定。共同信号是：Agent 正从开放域对话走向出错代价高的专业流程，隐私、权限与流程护栏成为论文核心设计议题，而非事后补丁。

趋势二：模型"来源审计"与"行为评测"双线并进，构成 AI 供应链基础设施。Witeness Overlap 实现开源权重模型的方向性溯源——不仅判断 checkpoint 是否相关，还能判定谁先谁后，回应"微调-合并-再发布"链条日益拉长的审计难题；ConflictVLA-Bench 则指出任务失败本身无法区分"指令前提矛盾"与"模型能力不足"，需要专门的行为评测。随着开源生态复杂化，溯源审计 + 前提冲突测试将成模型发布与采购的常规动作。
English
Trend 1: Agentic AI is penetrating safety-critical domains, with guardrails designed in from day one. SMARtCARE introduces a privacy-preserving, bounded-autonomy agent for clinical decision support—letting ICU monitoring AI reason over complete patient history so early vital-sign drift resembling a prior deterioration pattern is not dismissed as nonspecific. CP-Agent targets a different frontier: a harness-engineered agent that automates tool configuration, multi-step data pipelines, and constitutive-parameter calibration for crystal plasticity simulation. Both signal that agents are moving from open-domain chat into professional workflows where errors are costly, making privacy, permissions, and process guardrails first-class design concerns rather than afterthoughts.

Trend 2: Model provenance auditing and behavioral benchmarking are converging as AI supply-chain infrastructure. Witeness Overlap enables directional provenance inside open-weight model families—answering not merely whether checkpoints are related, but which came first, addressing the lengthening fine-tune/merge/re-release chain. ConflictVLA-Bench shows terminal task failure cannot distinguish invalid task premises from genuine capability gaps in vision-language-action models. As open ecosystems grow more complex, lineage audits plus premise-conflict behavior tests will become standard practice for model release and procurement.

1. 高危场景Agent前置设计权限分级与人工确认点——参考SMARtCARE的bounded-autonomy架构，比事后打补丁更稳妥。
2. 科研/工程多步流程是Agent落地蓝海——CP-Agent的harness-engineered思路可复用到材料、化工、生物信息等领域。
3. 为开源模型微调建立血缘台账——记录基座版本与派生关系，未来溯源审计可能成为合规要求。
4. 评测集加入"反常输入"用例——只看任务成功率不够，参考ConflictVLA-Bench测前提矛盾下的行为。
5. 做人机协作产品先画设计框架再写代码——Working with AI的方法论适合直接嵌入PRD评审环节。
English
1. Design permission tiers and human checkpoints up front for high-stakes agents—SMARtCARE's bounded-autonomy architecture is safer than retrofitted patches.
2. Multi-step scientific/engineering pipelines are a blue ocean for agents—CP-Agent's harness-engineered approach transfers to materials, chemistry, and bioinformatics.
3. Keep a lineage ledger for open-weight fine-tunes—logging base versions and derivatives prepares you for future provenance-audit compliance.
4. Add abnormal-input cases to your eval suite—task success rate alone is insufficient; benchmark premise-conflict behavior as ConflictVLA-Bench does.
5. Draft the human-AI collaboration framework before coding—Working with AI's methodology fits naturally into PRD review cycles.
