🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：Agentic AI 正在向高合规、高风险的垂直行业渗透。SMARtCARE 论文指出，ICU 监护中的长上下文临床 AI 容易遗漏历史入院记录，导致早期生命体征漂移被误判。该工作提出"有界自治"（bounded-autonomy）的隐私保护 Agent 架构，让 AI 在明确权限边界内辅助临床决策。这说明 Agent 的落地瓶颈已从"能力"转向"可信与合规"，医疗、金融等强监管行业将成为下一个主战场。

趋势二：开源模型的"血缘审计"成为新刚需。Witness Overlap 提出定向溯源方法，能判断同一家族开源模型中哪个 checkpoint 发布在先——这对模型合并、微调、再发布的合规审计至关重要。随着 Llama、Qwen 等衍生模型爆炸式增长，模型供应链的可追溯性将催生新的工具与商业机会。
English
Trend 1 — Agentic AI is penetrating highly regulated verticals. SMARtCARE identifies that long-context clinical AI in ICU monitoring misses prior admissions, causing early vital-sign drift to look nonspecific. Its solution: a privacy-preserving, bounded-autonomy agent architecture. The bottleneck for agents is shifting from capability to trust and compliance, making healthcare and finance the next battlegrounds.

Trend 2 — Provenance auditing of open-weight models is becoming essential. Witness Overlap introduces a directional method to determine which checkpoint in a model family came first, critical for auditing fine-tuning, merging, and re-release chains. As Llama/Qwen derivatives proliferate, model supply-chain traceability will create new tooling and business opportunities.
