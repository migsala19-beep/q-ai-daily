🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：AI Agent 基础设施进入"工程优化"阶段。两篇 arXiv 论文不约而同聚焦 agent 的连接与调用效率。《The Cost of Saying Hello》首次系统量化 agent 通信协议中的握手开销——定位对手、能力发现、会话建立都在首条有效消息前消耗延迟与 token；《MCP vs CLI for AI Agents》则对比两种工具集成范式：CLI 让 agent 直接执行 shell 命令，MCP 通过标准化协议接入文件、API 与云服务。两者共同指向：agent 生态正从"功能可用"转向"效率与成本可优化"，基础设施工程将成为下一阶段竞争焦点。

趋势二：AI 替代昂贵物理仿真（surrogate modelling）。加拿大野火研究用 AI 代理模型替代过程模拟框架，使全国性 hazard mapping 从"偶尔做"变为"频繁更新"，直接支撑燃料管理、基础设施保护与疏散路线决策。这代表了 AI for Science 的成熟路径：不是辅助分析，而是替代计算瓶颈本身。理论层面，《Neural Networks with Differentiable Belief-Function Layers》用 Yager 证据组合规则替代 Dempster 归一化，让反向传播在高冲突下更稳健，为可信 AI 提供新工具。
English
Two trends stand out today. First, agent infrastructure is entering an engineering-optimization phase. "The Cost of Saying Hello" provides the first empirical quantification of handshake overhead in agent communication protocols—locating counterparts, discovering capabilities, and establishing sessions all consume latency and tokens before the first useful message. "MCP vs CLI for AI Agents" compares the two competing tool-integration paradigms: agents executing shell commands directly versus standardized protocol-based access to files, APIs, and cloud services. Together they signal a shift from "agents that work" to "agents that are efficient and cost-optimizable."

Second, AI surrogate modelling is replacing expensive physical simulation. The Canadian wildfire study substitutes an AI surrogate for process-based simulation, turning national hazard mapping from an occasional exercise into a frequently updated operational capability that directly informs fuel management and evacuation routing. A theoretical companion: differentiable belief-function layers apply Yager's evidence-combination rule, redistributing mass to ignorance under high conflict—friendlier to backpropagation than Dempster normalization, and promising for trustworthy AI under uncertainty.

1. 为自有 Agent 系统做一次"握手审计"，复用论文1方法论定位延迟与 token 浪费点——连接成本常被忽略，却是规模化瓶颈。
2. 工具集成选型做 A/B 对比，勿默认 MCP——论文2显示 CLI 在部分场景更简单高效。
3. 盘点业务中的昂贵仿真/慢查询，评估 surrogate model 可行性——野火案例证明其可把离线分析变为在线服务。
4. 高冲突不确定场景（风控、医疗）关注可微信念函数层——冲突时倾向"无知"而非强行归一，比 softmax 更谨慎。
English
1. Run a "handshake audit" on your agent systems using Paper 1's methodology—connection cost is invisible but becomes the scaling bottleneck.
2. A/B-test tool integration instead of defaulting to MCP—Paper 2 shows CLI wins in several scenarios.
3. Inventory expensive simulations or slow queries in your business and evaluate surrogate models—the wildfire case proves offline analysis can become an online service.
4. For high-conflict uncertainty scenarios (risk control, healthcare), explore differentiable belief-function layers—they lean toward "ignorance" under conflict rather than forced normalization, behaving more cautiously than softmax.
