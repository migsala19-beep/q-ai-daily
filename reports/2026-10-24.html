🔬 深度研究报告

**趋势一：Agent 基础设施进入「效率实证」深水区。** 今日两篇论文共同指向一个信号：AI Agent 生态正从「协议能不能跑」转向「跑起来代价多大」。《The Cost of Saying Hello》首次系统量化 Agent 交互前的握手开销——定位对手、能力发现、会话建立都在消耗首条有效消息前的延迟预算；《MCP vs CLI》则对比了两大工具集成范式：MCP 标准化但引入额外协商成本，CLI 灵活但缺乏结构化契约。这表明 Agent 工程化已从概念验证进入性能调优阶段，类似当年微服务从「拆分」走向「Service Mesh 治理」。

**趋势二：不确定性推理的深度学习化复兴。** 《Neural Networks with Differentiable Belief-Function Layers》发现 Yager 1987 年提出的信念组合规则与反向传播天然契合——在高冲突场景下，质量应重分配至「无知」而非归一化到类别单纯形，这正是 Dempster 归一化在梯度传播中失效的解药。该方向对医疗诊断、自动驾驶等高风险决策场景意义重大，值得跟踪其代码开源情况。
English
**Trend 1: Agent infrastructure is entering the "efficiency empirics" phase.** Two papers signal a shift from "can protocols run" to "what do they cost." *The Cost of Saying Hello* provides the first systematic quantification of handshake overhead in agent communication—locating counterparts, capability discovery, and session establishment all consume latency budget before the first useful message. *MCP vs CLI* compares the two competing tool-integration paradigms: MCP offers standardization at the cost of negotiation overhead, while CLI offers flexibility without structured contracts. This mirrors the microservices evolution from "breaking monoliths" to "Service Mesh governance."

**Trend 2: A deep-learning revival of uncertainty reasoning.** *Differentiable Belief-Function Layers* reveals that Yager's 1987 rule of combination naturally fits backpropagation—under high conflict, mass should redistribute to ignorance rather than renormalize onto the class simplex, precisely fixing where Dempster's normalization fails during gradient propagation. This has significant implications for high-stakes decision-making in healthcare and autonomous driving.
