🔬 深度研究报告

今日资讯集中指向一个方向：AI 评估科学正在重构。一是 Agent 评估"实时化、可验证化"。《The World Won't Wait》提出 RealtimeBench（arXiv:2610.20665），含 180 个任务、六类游戏，核心论点是：在持续变化环境中，迟到的正确动作等同错误——推理与工具调用本身耗时，评测必须同时考察决策质量与决策时效，并据此教学 Agent 在时钟压力下行动。《AlphaDiana》（arXiv:2610.20675）进一步把 Agent 拆为"推理模型 + harness"（组织调用、执行工具、加载技能、维护记忆），强调测量要链接执行条件与结果、行为、资源消耗，服务可验证推理任务。二是"基准饱和"难题。《Evaluating Model Capabilities After Benchmark Saturation》（arXiv:2610.20667）指出：多模型逼近满分后基准失去区分度，直接削弱 AI 安全案例与前沿政策依据，必须发展饱和后仍可分辨能力的评估范式。工程侧亦有亮点：Value-Informed Schedule Trimming（arXiv:2610.20669）对离散扩散的逐步 SMC 引导做价值感知剪枝，降低奖励评估开销。
English
Today's papers point to one shift: evaluation science is being rebuilt. First, agent evaluation is becoming live and verifiable. "The World Won't Wait" (arXiv:2610.20665) introduces RealtimeBench with 180 tasks across six game types, arguing that in continuously changing environments a correct action that arrives late is still a failure—agents must be scored on both decision quality and decision latency. "AlphaDiana" (arXiv:2610.20675) models an agent as a reasoning model plus a harness (calls, tools, skills, memory), measuring how execution conditions drive outcomes, behavior, and resource use in verifiable reasoning. Second, benchmark saturation is now a first-class problem: once models near perfect scores, benchmarks lose discriminative power and weaken AI-safety cases and frontier policy (arXiv:2610.20667), requiring post-saturation evaluation paradigms. On the engineering side, value-informed schedule trimming (arXiv:2610.20669) prunes per-step SMC guidance in discrete diffusion, cutting reward-evaluation cost.

💡 实践启发

1. Agent 产品上线前测"延迟-正确率曲线"，而非只报准确率。RealtimeBench 表明实时环境中延迟本身即错误源。
2. 自建评测集设计防饱和机制：难度梯度、动态换题、保留未公开集。基准饱和会让安全评估失效。
3. 为 Agent 建立资源画像：记录 token 消耗、工具调用次数、时延并关联结果。AlphaDiana 显示执行条件与产出必须联合审计。
4. 审计日志保留中间证据链，勿只存最终正确结论。Verus 论文（arXiv:2610.20675 同批）提示正确性反馈可能擦除证据。
5. 测试时对齐场景关注 SMC trimming 类加速。奖励引导扩散/文生图对齐可显著降本。
English
1. Ship agents with a latency-accuracy curve, not just accuracy; RealtimeBench shows late correct actions still fail.
2. Build anti-saturation evals: difficulty tiers, rotating items, hidden test sets.
3. Profile agent resources (tokens, tool calls, latency) linked to outcomes for auditability.
4. Keep evidence chains in logs, not just final verdicts; feedback can erase intermediate proof.
5. Adopt SMC-trimming style acceleration for reward-aligned diffusion to cut inference cost.
