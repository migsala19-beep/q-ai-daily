English
No flagship model or AI framework trended today, but two quiet themes emerge. First, data sovereignty and decentralized tooling are heating up: yt-dlp is the workhorse for building proprietary audio/video corpora, Obtainium bypasses app stores to pull releases straight from upstream, and Technitium DNS reclaims network and privacy control. As training data and app distribution face tighter platform rules, developers are pulling the AI supply chain back into self-hosted infrastructure. Second, reproducible engineering is becoming the substrate: nixpkgs enables bit-for-bit reproducible environments, a direct answer to ML's replication crisis; runner-images harden CI consistency; and JUCE-class audio stacks supply the low-latency interface layer that on-device voice agents will need. Bottom line: no AI headline, but plenty of invisible foundation.
