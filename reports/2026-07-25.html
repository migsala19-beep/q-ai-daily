English
Two trends stand out. First, Agentic AI is moving from general-purpose chatbots into specialized, high-stakes domains. The AINTMA paper proposes a multi-agent architecture for autonomous test management, combining generative intelligence, secure cloud communication, and adaptive quality analytics. This reflects a broader shift toward agentic systems in DevOps and QA, where reliability and auditability matter. Second, LLM trustworthiness and content provenance are gaining urgency. A medical-text watermarking study warns that watermarking schemes validated on general benchmarks may fail in clinical settings, raising safety concerns. Meanwhile, ClickGuard applies LLMs to detect clickbait by measuring informativeness, showing a dual use of language models for both generating and judging content. Infrastructure signals also matter: PyTorch Geometric and Petals remain popular, indicating sustained developer interest in graph neural networks and decentralized, home-run LLM inference.
