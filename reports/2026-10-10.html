English
1. "Context slimming" for tool-using agents is an emerging research direction. The paper *Agent-Controlled Forgetting for Tool-Using Agents* lets agents autonomously replace old tool-result payloads with short notes, directly addressing context explosion and high token costs. Enterprises should prioritize context curation and verification mechanisms over longer reasoning chains.
