🔬 深度研究报告

今日可确认信号主要来自 arXiv，焦点集中在“企业级 Agent 如何可验证地落地”。其一，训练与评测数据正在走向合成化：Synthesis Through Simulation 指出，工具调用 Agent 受制于企业系统、数据与库 schema 的业务/法律限制，提出用可扩展的 Agent-系统交互生成连贯企业数据，本质是把真实企业流程搬进仿真沙盒做离线训练与回归评估。与之配套，Verification and Self-Improvement in Agentic AI 提醒：Agent 变好可能来自“搜得更久、外部支援更多、或改动提议与验证方式”，单一分数无法区分机制；只有带隐藏终止的有限验证才能拆解归因，避免把工程包装误认为能力跃迁。其二，上下文与数据质量成为可治理对象：Agent-Controlled Forgetting 让模型主动把旧工具结果替换成原位置短注且可逆，显著压缩 payload；Header-Centric STI 则把知识图谱质量前移到集成前的表头/列语义与数据质量评估。共同趋势是把“记忆、证据与元数据”做成可解释、可回滚的中间层，而非事后补救。
English
Today’s confirmed signals come mainly from arXiv and converge on verifiable enterprise-agent deployment. First, training/eval data is moving to synthesis: Synthesis Through Simulation argues tool-calling agents are constrained by business/legal limits on enterprise systems, data, and schemas, so scalable agent–system interaction should generate coherent synthetic enterprise data—effectively moving real workflows into a sandbox for offline training and regression tests. A companion paper, Verification and Self-Improvement in Agentic AI, warns that gains may come from longer search, extra support, or altered propose/verify loops; a single score cannot separate these mechanisms, so bounded verification with hidden termination is needed to attribute improvements and avoid mistaking scaffolding for capability. Second, context and data quality become governable layers: Agent-Controlled Forgetting replaces prior tool results with reversible short notes at their original slots, shrinking payloads; the header-centric STI paper pushes KG quality upstream into pre-integration header/column semantics and data-quality checks. The shared trend: make memory, evidence, and metadata explainable and reversible.
