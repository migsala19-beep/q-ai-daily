🔬 深度研究报告

趋势一：LLM 从"生成器"进化为"搜索引导者"。EliteTable 论文（arXiv:2610.12536）展示了新范式：不直接让 LLM 生成答案，而是用 LLM 引导程序搜索——维护一个固定容量的高分方案表，驱动新提案、方案编辑和多方案重组。这把 LLM 的创造力约束在可验证、可执行的搜索空间内，特别适合控制策略这类需要可执行轨迹的任务。结合同日论文"Know What You Don't Know"（arXiv:2610.12593）提出的智能体数据合成闭环，可以看出研究界正在解决"合成数据多样性枯竭"的痛点：让 Agent 主动探索"不知道"的空间，再用执行结果自我验证。

趋势二：可解释AI的评测危机。《Faithfulness Without Anchors》（arXiv:2610.12493）是一篇立场论文，直指十年来的特征重要性指标存在结构性错位——XAI 工具承诺提升信任，但证据表明这些忠实度度量本身并不可靠。这提醒从业者：在高风险场景（医疗、金融、合规）依赖 SHAP/LIME 类解释时，应警惕解释本身的正确性，而非仅信任模型输出。
English
Trend 1 — LLMs are evolving from generators into search guides. EliteTable (arXiv:2610.12536) keeps a fixed-capacity table of high-scoring solutions and uses an LLM to drive new proposals, edits, and remixes. This constrains LLM creativity within a verifiable, executable search space — ideal for control policies. Combined with "Know What You Don't Know" (arXiv:2610.12593), which proposes agentic data synthesis that actively explores unknown regions in a closed verification loop, the field is clearly addressing the synthetic-data diversity collapse problem.

Trend 2 — A crisis in XAI evaluation. "Faithfulness Without Anchors" (arXiv:2610.12493) argues that feature-importance faithfulness metrics suffer structural misalignment: a decade of XAI tools promised trust, yet accumulated evidence shows the metrics themselves are unreliable. Practitioners in high-stakes domains should audit their explanation methods, not just their models.

💡 实践启发

1. 在 RL/控制类任务中尝试"LLM引导搜索表"架构 —— 比纯端到端生成更稳定，方案可复用、可审计。
2. 做合成数据时引入"未知空间探索"机制 —— 避免模型只在自己熟悉的分布里自我重复，提升数据多样性。
3. 用 SHAP/LIME 做业务解释前，先小规模验证解释指标本身的忠实度 —— 论文表明这些指标可能结构性失准。
4. 企业内部 prompt 管理可评估 prompts.chat 自托管方案 —— 完全私有，适合有合规要求的团队。
5. 关注 Rust 生态工具链（egui、nautilus_trader）— AI 工程化的基础设施正在向高性能语言迁移。
English
1. Try the "LLM-guided solution table" pattern for RL/control tasks — more stable than end-to-end generation, with reusable, auditable solutions.
2. Add unknown-space exploration to synthetic data pipelines — prevents models from recycling their own familiar distribution.
3. Before using SHAP/LIME for business explanations, validate the faithfulness of the explanation metrics themselves — they may be structurally misaligned.
4. Evaluate self-hosted prompts.chat for enterprise prompt management — fully private, suitable for compliance-driven teams.
5. Watch the Rust tooling ecosystem (egui, nautilus_trader) — AI engineering infrastructure is migrating toward high-performance languages.
