🔬 深度研究报告

今日 arXiv 资讯呈现两条值得关注的线索。

其一，"agent 自我进化"正从概念走向可工程化。《The Trinity》提出能力驱动的递归自我改进框架，针对多模态智能体 scaling 的两大瓶颈——异构可验证环境构建成本高、人工 Harness 迭代慢——给出系统化方案。这与 CohortFlow 的思路形成呼应：后者将临床队列筛选中的自然语言修正表示为局部更新，避免重复处理未变更的需求，体现了"可操纵、可增量修正"成为 agent 落地医疗等严谨场景的刚需。共同趋势是：agent 设计正从"一次性完成任务"转向"持续演进、最小代价纠错"。

其二，对 AI 生产力的"冷思考"兴起。《Counting the Bandages》区分"秒表视角"与"账本视角"，指出 AI 节省的时间必须与它放行的错误并置评估——当 AI 生成的代码引入隐性 bug，省下的时间可能加倍偿还。这与《Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It》共同构成元层面的反思：当 AI 在某些任务上优于人类，人类研究者应重新定位自身角色，研究"如何衡量 AI"本身成为新课题。
English
Two notable threads emerge from today's arXiv feed.

First, agent self-evolution is becoming engineerable. "The Trinity" proposes a capability-driven recursive self-improvement framework addressing two scaling bottlenecks for multimodal agents: costly construction of heterogeneous verifiable environments and slow manual harness iteration. CohortFlow echoes this philosophy—representing natural-language corrections as localized updates during clinical cohort curation, avoiding reprocessing unchanged requirements. The shared trend: agent design is shifting from "one-shot task completion" toward "continuous evolution with minimal-cost correction."

Second, a wave of critical reflection on AI productivity. "Counting the Bandages" contrasts a stopwatch view with a ledger view—time saved by AI must be read next to the errors it lets through. When AI-generated code introduces subtle bugs, saved time may be repaid with interest. This pairs with "Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It": as AI surpasses humans in certain tasks, researchers must redefine their roles, and measuring AI itself becomes a research topic.

💡 实践启发

1. 评估团队 AI 工具 ROI 时，建立"账本"而非"秒表"——把返工和错误修复时间计入总账，避免被表面效率误导。
2. 设计 agent 工作流时预留"局部修正"接口——借鉴 CohortFlow，让需求变更只触发增量更新，降低迭代成本。
3. 垂直领域数据不足时，优先考虑数据生成策略——如橄榄树病害分类所示，合成数据可显著改善类别不平衡问题。
4. 关注递归自我改进的技术动态——The Trinity 类框架若成熟，将重塑 agent 开发范式，提前布局相关技能。
5. 思考 AI 时代个人研究/工作的不可替代性——参考"AI 更擅长科学时如何做科学"，尽早向提问、验证、跨域整合方向迁移。
English
1. Track AI tool ROI with a "ledger" not a "stopwatch"—factor in rework and bug-fixing time to avoid misleading efficiency claims.
2. Build "localized correction" interfaces into agent workflows—following CohortFlow, let requirement changes trigger only incremental updates.
3. When vertical-domain data is scarce, prioritize data generation strategies—synthetic data measurably improves class imbalance, as shown in the olive disease study.
4. Monitor recursive self-improvement developments—frameworks like The Trinity could reshape agent development paradigms; build relevant skills early.
5. Reflect on your irreplaceable role in the AI era—per "Why and How to Do Science When AI Is Better at It," shift toward questioning, verification, and cross-domain synthesis.
